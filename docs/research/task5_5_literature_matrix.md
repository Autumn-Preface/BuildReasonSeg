## 3. Per-work notes

Fact sources: `[F §n]` = `foundational.md` section n, `[S §n]` = `sa2va.md` section n, `[R §n]` = `rs_reasoning.md` section n. URLs are quoted as printed in those files. Every claim marked **[U]** is `UNVERIFIED` in the raw files.

**LISA** — `[F §2.1]`, `[F §7]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00692v1, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Lai_LISA_Reasoning_Segmentation_via_Large_Language_Model_CVPR_2024_paper.html)
- The language feature reaches the mask head as **the last-layer hidden embedding of the single `<SEG>` token** — `h̃_seg` → MLP `γ` (channels `[256, 4096, 4096]`) → `h_seg` — combined with dense `F_enc` features by the SAM decoder: `M̂ = F_dec(h_seg, f)`. This is the canonical "embedding-as-mask" path.
- Frozen: **the whole vision backbone `F_enc`** and nothing else; the LLM carries **LoRA**, the decoder `F_dec` is **fully fine-tuned**, and the LLM word embeddings plus `γ` are trainable.
- Loss: `L = λ_txt·L_txt + λ_mask·L_mask` with `L_mask = λ_bce·BCE + λ_dice·DICE` and weights **1.0 / 1.0 / 2.0 / 0.5**. There is no reference-region term.
- Reported cost: **8× RTX 3090 24 GB, 10,000 steps, < 3 days for LISA-7B**; DeepSpeed is stated; FlashAttention is **[U]**.
- Surprising: ReasonSeg contains only **1,218 image–instruction–mask samples** (239/200/779) and the images come from **OpenImages + ScanNetv2**, not aerial imagery — so RS transfer is untested in the original work.
- Licence warning: even the *paper* licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, v3) does not transfer to code, weights or ReasonSeg.

**LISA++** — `[F §2.2]`, `[F §5 #2]` (https://senqiaoyang.com/publication/lisa++/)
- The mask mechanism is **explicitly unchanged**: "without structural change and additional data sources", "keeping the base architecture intact"; the `<SEG>`-embedding → MLP `γ` → SAM-style decoder path is inherited, with **bipartite MaskFormer/DETR-style matching** for instance masks.
- The paper's real contribution is prompt/schema: verbatim SemSeg / InstSeg / SiD / Pure-Conversation templates that tell the model how to interpret masks — a *decoupled prompting* idea that reappears later in PSALM's task-instruction schema.
- New data is instruction-only: **ReasonSeg-Inst = 62K image–query pairs / 1.8K eval**, built from COCO and ADE20K with no new visual data.
- **Venue correction (preserve): LISA++ was never published at a venue.** The first author's own page classifies it Type: Preprint, Publication: Tech Report; arXiv comments contain only "Typo fixed". Calling it "CVPR 2024 LISA++" is wrong (`[F §5 #2]`).
- No repo URL is printed in its metadata, and both the encoder/decoder wording and the GPU count are **[U]** from LISA++ itself.

**GSVA** — `[F §2.3]`, `[F §4]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2312.10103v3)
- Language → mask: `h_seg = ψ(ỹ_txt[SEG])`, an MLP `ψ` projecting **each** `[SEG]` output embedding into the SFM prompt space as `R^{N_seg×C}`; then `ỹ_mask = F_mask(h_seg, f_seg)` decodes **one query per mask**. Multiple `[SEG]` tokens are weight-sharing, so multi-target output is a token-count decision, not a new module.
- `[REJ]` is the first explicit "no such object" mechanism in this line: rejected tokens are **discarded before decoding and assigned all-zero masks**. This is the precedent for empty-target handling.
- Frozen: **SAM ViT-H image encoder `F_V2` frozen, mask decoder `F_mask` fully trainable**; the MLLM LoRA status is **[U]**.
- Spatial relations are handled **implicitly** — the paper says GRES "increases complexity… by involving complicated spatial relationships" and that the "model has to learn to handle this spatial information"; there is **no relation module**.
- **Name collision:** "GSVA" is also the Bioconductor *Gene Set Variation Analysis* R package; always qualify as `GSVA (CVPR 2024, LeapLabTHU)` (`[F §5 #4]`).
- Licence: code, weights and gRefCOCO all `UNVERIFIED / DO NOT USE UNTIL RESOLVED`.

**PixelLM** — `[F §2.4]`, `[F §5 #6/#7/#8]` (https://pixellm.github.io/, https://arxiv.org/html/2312.02228v3)
- The **only surveyed general work with no external segmenter**: the mask is produced by `D`, L attention blocks consuming scale-matched image features, with the final mask `M̂ = Σ_ℓ γ^ℓ m^ℓ`. This is the "no SAM" alternative route.
- Language → mask: last-layer hidden embeddings of **learnable codebook tokens** `h = {h^ℓ}`; a linear projection `φ` fuses the N grouped tokens per scale; those fused embeddings are the queries.
- Attention is multiplicative and gated: `f_img^{ℓ'} = f_img^ℓ ⊙ (σ(m^{ℓ+1})+1)` — a coarse-to-fine refinement rather than a single-shot query.
- Loss includes a **target refinement loss** `L_ref` = α-weighted per-pixel BCE on pixels where **≥2 targets overlap**; the arXiv abstract names only this loss while the project page also names a "token fusion method" — the two artifacts describe the loss differently (`[F §5 #7]`).
- Frozen: **CLIP encoder and LLM frozen (LLM carries LoRA)**; projections, codebook and pixel decoder trainable.
- **MUSE caution:** PixelLM's MUSE is **246k QA / 0.9M instances**; HF `dongxiaoyu/MUSE` (106 cases) is a different artifact (`[F §5 #6]`).

**GLaMM** — `[F §2.5]`, `[F §4]`, `[F §5 #14]` (https://mbzuai-oryx.github.io/groundingLMM/)
- Structurally the richest of the general works: five components — Global Image Encoder, **Region Encoder**, LLM, Grounding Image Encoder, Pixel Decoder — and the **Region Encoder lets a *second region* be supplied as an optional visual prompt** in addition to text. That is the closest general-purpose thing to a reference-region input, but it is **user-supplied, not model-predicted, and produces no reference-mask output or supervision**.
- Its own literal token strings, base-LLM identity, losses and frozen/trainable recipe are all **[U]** — the loss section fell beyond the retrievable truncation, and an AI-generated summary that claimed `<image>`/`<bbox>`/`<SEG>`/`<p>`/`</p>` tokens and an "L-P projection" was **deliberately excluded** as non-primary (`[F §8]`).
- GranD's annotation schema is the closest thing to structured spatial supervision in this generation: level-2 explicitly includes "**relational markers**" / "Relationships and Landmarks"; level-3 "builds a scene graph". Note this is **annotation structure, not a mechanism or a loss**.
- Two-stage training GranDp → GranDf is stated; GPU type/count/epochs **[U]**. `grounding-anything.com` returned **HTTP 503** (`[F §8]`).
- The authors' "first model that can generate natural language responses seamlessly intertwined with corresponding object segmentation masks" is **their attributed claim**, not an asserted fact (`[F §5 #14]`).
- **Lineage correction:** "GLaMM-2" does not exist in any primary source; the video descendant is VideoGLaMM (`[F §5 #10/#11]`).

**PSALM** — `[F §2.6]`, `[F §4]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14598v1)
- **The `[REF]` name collision that matters.** PSALM defines a "special `[REF]` token, which is appended after the condition sentence as an anchor to aggregate useful information", and **its output embedding is used as the classifier weight**. It is a *sentence-condition aggregator*, **not** a second referent region and carries **no reference-mask supervision**. Any new `[REF]` design must be explicitly distinguished from this one (`[F §5 (C)]`).
- Mask generation is **decoupled propose-then-classify**: 100 learnable mask tokens + Swin multi-level features + condition embeddings → MaskGenerator → N proposals; classification uses pooled category-name embeddings, the `[REF]` embedding, or pooled visual-prior features.
- Frozen/trainable is **staged**: stage 1 freezes the vision encoder *and* the LMM; stage 2 freezes *only* the vision encoder and fine-tunes everything including the mask generator.
- Cost is the best-documented of the general set: **16× V100, 56k iterations, batch 64, AdamW lr 4e-5 cosine with no weight decay, 1024² input, two stages** (CC3M alignment → joint training on COCO-Pan, RefCOCO/+/g, COCO-Interactive, LLaVA-1.5 data).
- Efficiency framing: the authors adopt Phi-1.5 1.3B because they are "limited by resources" — a data point that a small-LLM route is viable.
- **Name collision:** "psalm" is also a PHP static-analysis tool; qualify as `PSALM (ECCV 2024, zamling/PSALM)` (`[F §5 #9]`).

**READ** — `[F §5(A)]`, `[F §3.7]`, `[F §4]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17741)
- The mechanism is **diagnosis then repair**: similarity maps computed between the `<SEG>` token and image-token embeddings at the last hidden layer of **both** the LLaVA encoder and the SAM decoder show "a striking consistency… in terms of activation responses", leading the authors to conclude the token performs **semantic similarity matching between text and image patches**.
- The repair is the **Similarity-as-Points module (SasP)**: parameter-free dot-product similarity → mean/σ thresholds → positive/negative/neutral points → **Gaussian-like weighted-average interpolation makes the coordinates differentiable** → points plus the `<SEG>` embedding feed SAM's prompt encoder/decoder, and the mask loss back-propagates into the LMM.
- Note the conceptual limit: differentiability (`DtoC`) is **only a gradient pathway, not a consistency constraint** (`[F §4]`).
- Single `<SEG>` only; **no `[REF]`, no `[REJ]`, no relation encoder, no consistency loss**. Benchmarks: ReasonSeg, RefCOCO(+/g), augmented FP-RefCOCO(+/g) for forgetting.
- **Seed correction (preserve):** the seed title "READ: Referring Expression Comprehension and Segmentation with Large Language Models" **does not exist** (no arXiv/OpenAlex/Crossref match); the survey's READ-at-CVPR-24 row is a probable venue error → **[U]** (`[F §5(A)]`).
- Base LLaVA size, SAM variant, GPUs, data scale and all licences are **[U]**; the ~55-star / 2026-02-04 figure is **secondary** and not authoritative.

**SAMTok** — `[F §3.7]`, `[S §1]`, `[S §3]`, `[S §6]` (https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhou_SAMTok_Representing_Any_Mask_with_Two_Words_CVPR_2026_paper.html)
- Language → mask is **fully discretised**: a residual VQ turns a mask into **exactly two** code indices (`e1 = argmin‖z−e‖²`, `r1 = z−e1`, `e2 = argmin‖r1−e‖²`), and the **two code embeddings are summed into SAM's sparse prompt embeddings**, then cross-attended with image features. This is a mask *language*, not a continuous latent.
- **Masks can appear in the prompt**, i.e. a mask-in region reference exists at CVPR 2026 Highlight level; mask tokens are added to the MLLM vocabulary in a number equal to the codebook size (256×2). This makes "we add a reference-region token" **not inherently novel** (`[F §3.7]`).
- Training is next-token prediction plus **GRPO with a purely textual answer-matching reward** `R_mask = N_TP / max(N_pred, N_gt)`; the reconstruction objective is `CE + DICE + commitment`. The authors frame pixel capability as learned "without architectural modifications and specialized loss design".
- Scale: **209M masks** for the tokenizer, **5M** SAMTok-formatted samples for SFT. Base MLLMs: Qwen2.5-VL-3B/7B, Qwen3-VL-4B/8B, PLM-1B.
- **SAMTok is NOT the tokenizer inside the shipped Sa2VA checkpoints** — Sa2VA emits one `[SEG]` whose *continuous* last-layer hidden state is projected into SAM2 prompt space; the Sa2VA chat module contains no mask encoder, no VQ codebook and no `<|mt_*|>` tokens (`[S §3]`).
- Released literal token strings, SFT parameterisation, GPU counts and all licences are **[U]**; the CC BY-SA 4.0 statement applies to the **project website only**.

**OMG-LLaVA** — `[F §3.7]`, `[F §4]`, `[F §5 #5]`
- The `<Region>` token is replaced by **object-centric visual tokens encoded from a user point/box/mask visual prompt** — a de-facto explicit reference-region input, but **not** a text-vocabulary token, and there is **no reference-mask output or supervision**.
- Perceptual fusion is explicit and named: **Perception Prior Embedding** `MS = Softmax(M ⊙ S)`, `T_pv = MS·Q + F` — one of only two works in the general set with an explicitly named fusion block (`[F §4]`).
- Two-stage training with a deliberate freeze order: pretraining freezes perception **and** LLM (projectors only); instruction tuning adds **LoRA on the LLM**.
- The frozen OMG decoder's self-attention "models relationships between objects", but those relations are **query-to-query, not over the geometry of predicted regions**.
- Supports **video** (VIPSeg video panoptic/VPQ) in addition to images — one of the few general works that does.
- **Name correction (preserve): "OMGM" does not exist.** The real pair is OMG-Seg (CVPR 2024, arXiv 2401.10229) and OMG-LLaVA (NeurIPS 2024) (`[F §5 #5]`).

**Text4Seg** — `[F §3.7]`, `[F §4]` (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2410.09855)
- Mechanism is **"text-as-mask", decoder-free**: a 16×16 grid of 256 semantic descriptors maps each image patch to a text label; masks are recovered by de-tokenising the descriptor grid. **Row-wise Run-Length Encoding** (`|` for columns, `\n` for rows) is reported to cut descriptor length by 74 % and give 3× faster inference.
- The only segmentation-side network is an optional off-the-shelf **SAM ViT-H** (or CRF) post-hoc refiner — this is the clearest "ordinary semantic segmentation with a language front-end" in the set.
- Breadth is unusual for this line: **LLaVA-1.5-7B, DeepSeek-VL-1.3B/7B, Qwen-VL-7B, InternVL2-8B** with one recipe.
- Cost is reported: **LoRA r=64, 8× Tesla A800 40 GB, global batch 128, AdamW lr 2e-4, DeepSpeed ZeRO-2 via the SWIFT framework**.
- No mask/decoder token, no `[REF]`, no `[REJ]`; the **plain autoregressive text loss is the only training signal** (no Dice/BCE, no consistency term).

**SegLLM** — `[F §3.1]`, `[F §4]`, `[R §2]`, `[R §7 Q1–Q5]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2)
- **The decisive collision.** `[REF]` is explicitly defined as "the referenced mask" and `[SEG]` as "the relational query"; the two-query loss `L_mask = L_seg(F([REF],[PAD]), M_ref) + L_seg(F([REF],[SEG]), M_tgt)` with `L_seg = L_CE + λ·L_DICE` (λ = 1) means **the reference mask itself is supervised**. A reference-region token with reference-mask supervision has existed since Oct 2024.
- The geometry of predicted regions re-enters the model: a **CLIP-ViT embedding of the masked, background-blackened object crop** plus a **bounding-box positional embedding computed from the generated mask** are concatenated into the LLM input stream for the next round.
- But relations are still resolved **implicitly by the LLM over text/embedding context**; there is **no dedicated Spatial Relation Encoder module** — this is the note-taker's analytical judgement from the mechanism description, not a claim the SegLLM authors make (`[F §4]`).
- Relation taxonomy is the richest surveyed: **positional, interactional, hierarchical**; template "Can you segment the `<class>` that is `<relationship>` the output from round `<i>`?"; chaining happens **across conversational rounds (2–8)**, not as ≥2 relations composed inside one instruction.
- Frozen: **CLIP image encoder and HIPIE-R50 mask decoder frozen**; LLM and projector `f_V2L` fine-tuned. Cost: **A100 GPUs (count not stated), total batch 16, AdamW lr 2e-5, DeepSpeed stage-2, bf16**.
- **Venue/status (preserve): arXiv preprint with no venue stated; the survey's Table 1 independently lists SegLLM as `<SEG>, <REF>`. The paper has an appendix section titled "License" whose content was not retrievable (`[R §9.1]`).**

**Sa2VA** — `[S §1]`, `[S §5]`, `[S §6]`, `[S §9]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2501.04001v4)
- Language → mask at code level: `generate(..., output_hidden_states=True)` → `get_seg_hidden_states(last_hidden_states, sequences[0][:-1], seg_id=self.seg_token_idx)` selects the `[SEG]` rows → `text_hidden_fcs` (an `nn.Sequential`, in_dim→out_dim MLP with ReLU) → `SAM2.language_embd_inference(...)`. Training-side equivalent: `seg_token_mask = input_ids == self.seg_token_idx`; `pred_embeddings = hidden_states[seg_token_mask]`.
- **A downstream module cannot get the `[SEG]` hidden state from the public API** — `predict_forward` returns only `{'prediction', 'prediction_masks'}`; a consumer must re-implement the ~15-line extraction routine or call `model.model.generate(..., output_hidden_states=True)` itself. No documented hook/callback exists.
- Frozen: `freeze_llm=True`, `freeze_visual_encoder=True`, `frozen_sam2_decoder=False`; **LoRA on the LLM only**; the whole grounding encoder is frozen and then **only `sam2_model.sam_mask_decoder` is unfrozen**; the saved state dict keeps only that decoder, `text_hidden_fcs.*` and the MLLM.
- Cost/reproducibility wall: one stage, `max_epochs = 1`, effective batch **128 = 1×8×16 GPUs**, "at least 8 A100 GPUs", and a **418.8 GB training bundle with no licence field mirrored**. The published §Implementation Details (GPUs/hours/epochs) is **[U]** (fetch truncation) — the repo-side numbers must not be attributed to the paper.
- Adding a second token is a **config edit, not a code change** (`special_tokens` → `tokenizer.add_tokens(..., special_tokens=True)` → `resize_token_embeddings`), and a downstream fine-tune template exists (VRT, LoRA r=128 from a converted checkpoint). But "add `[REF]` to a released checkpoint **without retraining**" is **[U]** — the new embedding row is random.
- Runtime is hostile to the target platform: `flash-attn==2.7.3` is a hard pinned compiled CUDA extension, mask decoding calls `torch.autocast(device_type="cuda")`, and **no Windows support is documented** (CPU-only torch index off-Linux, `mp_start_method='fork'`). Documented inference VRAM is **[U]**.
- **Licence:** every artefact is `UNVERIFIED / DO NOT USE UNTIL RESOLVED`; the repo `LICENSE` text observed through a proxy is **Apache-2.0 but inadmissible**; the **SAM3 variant carries `license: other` + gated** on mirror metadata and is flagged as the family's highest licensing risk. There is a **different base-model licence per variant** (Qwen3-VL / Qwen2.5-VL `qwen-research` / InternVL MIT / LLaVA `llama2` implied) — a real multi-layer risk.

**VideoGLaMM** — `[F §3.8]`, `[F §2.5]` (https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Munasinghe_VideoGLaMM__A_Large_Multimodal_Model_for_Pixel-Level_Visual_Grounding_CVPR_2025_paper.html)
- The **actual GLaMM-line video descendant** (CVPR 2025, arXiv 2411.04923, v1 2024-11-07) — it, not Sa2VA, is the work to cite for the GLaMM lineage (`[F §5 #10]`).
- Token scheme: **`<p>` … `</p>` grounding tags plus `<SEG>`**; the architecture is GLaMM "augmenting its architecture with **SAM2**".
- Its token scheme is also the **strongest indirect evidence** for what GLaMM itself probably does — but it is not a direct GLaMM confirmation.
- Contribution is pixel-level visual grounding in video; there is **no spatial-relation mechanism**.

**PaDT** — `[F §3.5]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01954)
- **Visual Reference Tokens (VRTs)** are "visual patch embeddings of query images… interleaved with LLM's output textual tokens"; code at `github.com/Gorilla-Lab-SCUT/PaDT`.
- Mechanically these are **output-side visual tokens, not a token denoting a second referent region** → ORTHOGONAL in mechanism, but the *term* "reference token" is already occupied.
- Consequence for any new design: the `[REF]` semantics and the phrase "reference token" must both be defined explicitly or a distinct name chosen.
- Naming hazard: the Sa2VA monorepo also ships a project called **VRT** (`projects/vrt_sa2va`, "Visual Reasoning Tracer"), which is a *different* artifact from PaDT's VRTs (`[S §6]`, `[S §13]`). Do not conflate the two.

**PhysMLLMs** — `[F §3.6]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24574)
- Injects physics-inspired **spatial continuity priors** via **Global Representation Prior Alignment (REPA-Global)**, distilling a **frozen DINOv2 teacher** with an offline embedding cache.
- The target is **video spatio-temporal consistency** — jitter, drift and identity switches — **not** per-instruction agreement between predicted geometry and stated spatial relations. Mechanically ORTHOGONAL.
- It matters only because it shows the phrase **"spatial consistency" is already claimed in this neighbourhood**: any new consistency loss must state its exact definition and its target explicitly.
- **UNVERIFIED:** all architecture specifics other than the distillation scheme; repo URL attempted but unreachable.

**R²S / 3D ReasonSeg** — `[F §3.8]` (https://iccv.thecvf.com/virtual/2025/poster/2398)
- Two-stage: first identify **relevant elements**, then segment guided by **their visual priors (3D point clouds)**.
- This is a genuine antecedent-region-conditions-target design and therefore **PARTIAL_OVERLAP on a reference-component concept** — but via point-cloud priors and **without a reference token**, and with no reference-mask supervision.
- Venue is first-hand (ICCV 2025 program); all architecture, data, cost and licence cells are **[U]**.

**VLM-FO1** — `[F §3.8]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25916)
- Contribution: a **Hybrid Fine-grained Region Encoder** plus a **token-based referencing system** letting the LLM reason about specific visual regions.
- Critically, this is **not a segmentation model**, and the raw note records **no evidence of a dedicated second-region token conditioning a mask** → PARTIAL_OVERLAP on the reference-token concept only.
- Everything else (base model, encoder details, data, cost, licence) is **[U]**.

**SATURN** — `[F §3.8]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22694)
- Neuro-symbolic **soft perspective-aware spatial predicates** composed by a **symbolic executor** for multi-hop inference; evaluated on the 3D FORCE benchmark.
- Closest surveyed thing to **explicitly represented, re-checkable relations** — but it is **grounding, not masks**, and it "preserves uncertainty across hops but defines no geometric-consistency loss" (`[F §4]`).
- It therefore supports the gap claim that multi-hop spatial composition exists *outside* the segmentation line while being absent *inside* it.

**SegEarth-R1** — `[R §1]`, `[R §4]`, `[R §5]`, `[R §7]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09644v1)
- Language → mask has **no special token at all**. The `<DESCRIPTION>` embeddings are **averaged into one global vector**, cross-attended with flattened multi-scale visual features, passed through a skip-connection + linear layer to yield **a single query vector**, and decoded by a Mask2Former decoder whose mask-query mechanism and bipartite matching were **removed** (focal + dice loss).
- The prompt is a fixed English template: `"USER: This is an image <IMAGE>, please doing geospatial pixel reasoning according to the following instruction: <DESCRIPTION>. ASSISTANT: <ANSWER>"` — the direct evidence that EarthReason's published interface is **English-only**.
- Encoding is deliberately compressed ("aggressive visual token compression" via stacked `Conv∘LN`), justified by an entropy + SSIM redundancy analysis — relevant because ultra-high-resolution RS inputs (up to 7617²) otherwise dominate memory.
- Data provenance is worth copying: masks annotated from scratch by RS experts with cross-validation (SAM-H assisting simple targets); QA generated with **GPT-4o** and rephrased with GPT-3.5.
- Freeze schedule, base-LLM identity, training cost and runtime deps are all **[U]**; the repo is unreachable, and the project page (a `github.io` host) is corroboration-only under `[R §0]`.
- **EarthReason is a dataset, not a method** — a fact the raw files state explicitly because the seed treated it as a candidate method (`[R §1]`).

**EarthReason** — `[R §1]`, `[R §3]`, `[R §7]` (https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/earthreason)
- Dataset released with SegEarth-R1: **5,434 manually annotated image masks, >30,000 implicit QA pairs, 28 categories, 0.5–153 m, 123²–7617²; splits 2,371 / 1,135 / 1,928**.
- The IEEE DataPort record (DOI 10.21227/2gsy-8b70, created/last-updated **2025-09-23**) states **"Files have not been uploaded for this dataset"** — an availability problem independent of licensing.
- Its queries are **implicit scene questions**, not relations to a reference region; every later RS paper (Think2Seg-RS, FIRM, RemoteReasoner) reports on it, so it is the incumbent benchmark to beat.
- Dataset licence is `UNVERIFIED / DO NOT USE UNTIL RESOLVED`.

**SegEarth-R2 (+ LaSeRS)** — `[R §2]`, `[R §4]`, `[R §7 Q1/Q4]`, `[R §10.1]` (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.20013)
- Adds the surveyed line's **only explicit spatial supervision loss**: `L_S = -log( Σ(A_S − a)²·Ĝ / ΣĜ )`, maximising separation between `[SEG]`→patch attention on foreground and the average background attention `a`, with `Ĝ` the **downsampled GT mask**. It is **attention-space, not geometry-space**.
- Token design is target-only: **`[SEG]` queries, no reference token** — so the RS-specific reference-token slot remains open while the generic mechanism (SegLLM) is taken (`[R §7 Q2]`).
- The "flexible segmentation-query mechanism" covers single- *and* multi-target prediction, i.e. multi-target is a query-mechanism property rather than a separate head.
- LaSeRS provenance is unusually well documented: **40,396 masks, 30,830 QA–mask triples, 122 categories, 1,900 held-out test masks, 4 evaluation dimensions**, masks from **SAMRS + SAM grid prompts**, QA by **Gemini-2.5-pro**, and **manual review of all QA**.
- The paper states **"All data and code will be released."**, but **no weights were located in any reachable source** → `UNVERIFIED` (`[R §9.1]`).
- Base-MLLM identity (a "3B MLLM") and the freeze map are **[U]**.

**Think2Seg-RS** — `[R §1.1]`, `[R §4]`, `[R §5]`, `[R §7 Q1]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19302v2)
- The mechanism deliberately **avoids a latent `[SEG]` embedding**: the LVLM emits **explicit geometric coordinates** (one bbox + two positive points per instance, JSON inside `<answer>`, CoT inside `<think>`), which are parsed and executed by **frozen SAM2**, then unioned `M = ⋃_i M(i)`. The stated rationale is to bypass the "`[SEG]`-embedding information bottleneck".
- Empty-target handling is explicit and format-safe: an empty list `[]` keeps the JSON valid.
- Supervision is **mask-only GRPO**: reward = format reward (valid `<think>`/`<answer>` + JSON) **+ final mask IoU**; GRPO with clip `ε` and KL penalty `β·D_KL(π_θ‖π_ref)`. This is the surveyed line's nearest existing "geometry-verifiable" supervision — but it verifies the **final mask against the GT mask**, never the relation (`[R §7 Q1]`).
- Frozen: **LVLM is the only trainable component; SAM2 is frozen** (explicit in the paper).
- Results: trains on EarthReason; zero-shot on RRSIS-D / RISBench / RefSegRS; EarthReason test **cIoU 75.60 / gIoU 73.36** (+6.47 / +2.40 over the strongest baseline).
- **Bibliographic caution (preserve):** the journal **name** is first-party confirmed from the arXiv journal line; **volume 237 / pages 217–235 / DOI 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.04.036 rest on a blackholed author page → `UNVERIFIED` pending publisher confirmation**. `[R §1.1]` rules: cite the paper, do **not** ship the code or weights until licences are read first-hand.

**FIRM** — `[R §1]`, `[R §4]`, `[R §7 Q1]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13980v1)
- Replaces the decoder with a **discrete mask-code readout**: `[SEG]` hidden state `z_k` conditions N `[MASK]` queries via `e_{k,j} = e_mask + W_t z_k + W_v v_j`; one parallel pass produces code posteriors, argmax gives an `r×r` sub-cell mask from a **fixed lookup table** (`2^{r²}` codes), marginalization gives a soft field, and **PACR** adds a residual with a **zero-initialised last layer**.
- Cross-target leakage is prevented structurally: **attention between different targets' `[MASK]` queries is blocked** — a small but concrete multi-target design decision.
- The GT mask codes are a **deterministic geometric function of the GT mask** (sub-cell occupancy ≥ **τ = 0.5**), which makes them re-checkable — the reason `[R §7 Q1]` lists FIRM among the "partially geometry-verifiable" works. It is still **reconstruction fidelity, not relation verification**.
- Granularity is an explicit hyper-parameter: `r=2` → 16 codes, `r=3` → 512 codes.
- Results span five benchmarks (satellite + UAV): **LaSeRS 70.5/80.5 gIoU/cIoU, EarthReason +3.0 avg, DRSeg, RRSIS-D, RISBench**.
- Code URL is asserted in the paper but **no weights were located** → early release; no venue is stated.

**TerraScope** — `[R §2]`, `[R §4]`, `[R §5]`, `[R §7 Q5]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19039v1)
- The clearest implementation of "thinking with pixels": on each `[SEG]` the mask decoder predicts `m_i`, the mask is resized to the token grid, a token is selected only if the mask covers **>50 %** of its region, and the selected masked visual features are projected back into the LLM, which **resumes from the KV cache**.
- **Temporal disambiguation is explicit**: `Image: t_i` indicators precede each `[SEG]` so the model knows which frame to segment and from which frame to sample features.
- Multi-hop is real but evaluative rather than mechanistic: **L2-Spatial** tasks "require cross-entity spatial analysis such as relationship inference" (e.g. "Is the water adjacent to the crops?"), built by **composing multiple L1 questions** into multi-step tasks, with masks interleaved in the trace.
- Training data is the largest surveyed RS reasoning corpus: **2 M referring-segmentation pairs → 1 M Terra-CoT** (250 K Cap-CoT + hierarchical synthesis). Masked features are taken from **GT masks** during training — a train/test asymmetry worth noting.
- Loss is simple: `L = L_LM + 0.5·L_seg`, `L_seg` = Dice + pixel-wise CE. **No consistency term.**
- Modality is broader than most: **optical + SAR + multi-temporal sequences**, not video.
- Sub-task labels CA/AQ/CR/BRD/DM/BCE are recorded but their **expansions are `UNVERIFIED`** (`[R §9.1]`).

**STAMP / STAMPlus** — `[R §2]`, `[R §4]`, `[R §5]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02791)
- Output interface, not a relation method: an in-vocabulary **`<SEG>` trigger token** followed by **"All-Mask Prediction" — a non-autoregressive pass with hybrid attention** that classifies all tokens foreground/background at once; no external decoder.
- STAMPlus adds **explicit target IDs and optional boxes bound to a shared multi-class mask space**, i.e. identity is carried in the output schema.
- Efficiency is the headline claim: **13.50 s → 5.16 s** for 12 categories.
- RS small-target segmentation is an **extension**, not the core setting; the exact RS datasets used are **[U]**.
- Weight artefact `JiaZL/STAMP-7B-lora` is mirror-only evidence; base MLLM, freeze map and training cost are **[U]**.

**PixDLM / DRSeg** — `[R §2]`, `[R §5]`, `[R §10.1]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15670)
- Contribution to this audit: it defines an **explicit "Spatial" reasoning dimension** for aerial reasoning segmentation and supervises it with **CoT QA**, alongside Attribute- and Scene-level dimensions.
- Data: **DRSeg — 10k high-resolution aerial images + CoT QA** across the three dimensions; this is the UAV analogue of EarthReason.
- Venue is first-hand (**CVPR 2026 highlight, pp. 26165–26175**), and the abstract states all datasets/models/code are at `https://github.com/XIEFOX/PixDLM` (repo unreachable).
- **Everything architectural is `UNVERIFIED`** — only the task definition and the data description are confirmed; in particular there is **no reference-region concept** reported.

**UniGeoSeg (+ GeoSeg-1M, GeoSeg-Bench)** — `[R §2]`, `[R §5]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23332)
- Scale baseline and pretraining-data source: **GeoSeg-1M = 590 K images, 117 categories, 1.1 M image–mask–instruction triplets** (automatic mask filtering + instruction generation).
- Its named components are a **task-aware text enhancement**, a **latent knowledge memory**, and **progressive multi-task training**; everything else is **[U]**.
- Its reasoning subset is **auto-generated instruction-driven segmentation**, not relation-to-reference-building reasoning.
- **Naming hazard (preserve): two different "GeoSeg-Bench" exist** — UniGeoSeg's (CVPR 2026, from GeoSeg-1M) and GeoSeg's (arXiv 2603.03983, 810 pairs). Do not conflate them in related work (`[R §10.2]`).

**TerraLogic** — `[R §2]`, `[R §5]`, `[R §7 Q5]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12497)
- **No masks.** 545 scenario-driven, **hierarchy-aware, long-horizon** geospatial reasoning tasks (hazard vulnerability, urban heat island, forest fragmentation, …) driven by a HieraPlan agent + tool hierarchy over optical, SAR and IR inputs.
- It is the strongest evidence that **multi-hop, hierarchical spatial reasoning is already benchmarked in EO** — which is why a multi-hop-relation claim must be paired with a *segmentation* mechanism and *geometric* verification to be defensible.
- Dataset/agent code is asserted but unreachable; no weights.

**OVEarth-Bench** — `[R §2]`, `[R §5]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27278)
- A 2026 zero-shot open-vocabulary EO benchmark whose query set includes **reasoning queries and negative queries**, with hierarchical category coverage of positive **and negative** expressions and a unified zero-shot protocol (mask + box metrics).
- Relevant as an extra evaluation suite and as prior art for **explicit negative-query evaluation**; it is not a method and is not relation- or reference-region specific.
- Dataset licence `UNVERIFIED / DO NOT USE UNTIL RESOLVED`.

**LISAt / GRES** — `[R §2]`, `[R §3]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02829)
- Early RS reasoning-segmentation VLM (UC Berkeley BAIR, 2025-05-05, 28 pp) with **GRES: 27,615 annotations / 9,205 images** and **PreGRES >1M QA**.
- Same task family and mask output as the rest of the RS line, but **no reference-region geometry or building-group design**.
- Architecture, base model, cost, dependencies and licences are all **[U]**; only the project page and dataset statistics are established.

**GeoSeg** — `[R §10.2]`, `[F §3.3]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03983)
- **Training-free and zero-shot.** Two named components: **bias-aware coordinate refinement** (corrects systematic grounding shifts) and a **dual-route prompting mechanism** that fuses semantic intent with fine-grained spatial cues. **Nothing is trained.**
- This is the closest 2026 work to "spatially-cued reasoning segmentation in remote sensing" and it explicitly claims semantic+spatial fusion — so any **semantic–spatial fusion** claim is contested and must be differentiated (`[F §3.3]`).
- Introduces **GeoSeg-Bench: 810 image–query pairs with hierarchical difficulty levels**.
- No repo or weights were located in reachable sources; the project page is a blackholed `github.io` host → **[U]**.
- Paper licence is **CC BY 4.0** (first-hand on the arXiv abs page) — **paper only**; code/weights are `n/a (training-free; no repo found)` and the dataset is `UNVERIFIED`.

**GeoSelect** — `[F §3.8]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03869)
- Referring segmentation is reframed as **execution of a typed spatial DSL over scored candidate sets**, composing **extremum / ordinal / top-k / relational** operators, with **every intermediate inspectable** — the most transparent spatial pipeline in the set.
- It is the **only work found that already does spatial-relation-guided segmentation in remote sensing, including chained ordinal+relational queries** (`[F §3.8]`) — but it is a **training-free program executor, not a learned relation encoder**, and it **consumes detected candidates rather than the model's own predicted component masks**.
- No reference-component token; not building-specific. Venue is first-hand (IEEE TGRS 2026, DOI 10.1109/TGRS.2026.3734378).

**GeoSearcher** — `[F §3.8]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01050)
- Explicitly notes that "queries often contain **reference objects**, spatial relations, and target attributes", and represents key clues as explicit **anchors**, progressively integrating location / relational / attribute clues — the closest prior art to a reference-component *concept*.
- Critical limit: it is **visual grounding, not segmentation** — no masks and **no reference-mask supervision**.
- Base model, data, cost and licence are **[U]**.

**RECS4R** — `[F §3.8]` (https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2026/poster/36204)
- Language-conditioned **unified contour decoding** for joint REC/RIS in RS, with **channel-isolated fusion**, residual coarse-to-fine refinement and a **gradient consistency loss**.
- Near-miss worth stating precisely: that "consistency loss" is a **boundary-gradient loss**, **not** a spatial-relation or geometry-consistency loss. It nevertheless crowds the *fusion* and *consistency-loss naming* slots.
- Venue verified first-hand (CVPR 2026 poster 36204); all other fields **[U]**.

**RSRefSeg** — `[F §3.8]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06809)
- CLIP global + local text semantics act as **filters** → visual activation → SAM prompts, evaluated on RRSIS-D.
- Classified **ORTHOGONAL**: it is **referring (explicit), not reasoning**, with **no spatial relations and no reference token**.
- **Seed correction (preserve):** the seed's "RS-RefSeg" is really **`RSRefSeg`** — the title as written in the seed is unverified (`[F §5 #12]`).

**RemoteReasoner** — `[R §2]`, `[R §5]`, `[R §8]` (https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/38175)
- AAAI 2026 (vol. 40 no. 14, art. 38175) unified geospatial reasoning workflow baseline; **content is paywalled**.
- The one mechanism detail available is second-hand, from Think2Seg-RS's related work: it applies **mask-to-box conversion on EarthReason for pseudo box–point supervision** — i.e. it *converts* masks into weaker geometry rather than verifying relations.
- Cited here only as a venue record; no architecture, cost or licence facts.

**GEAR-Seg** — `[F §3.8]` (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00544)
- A decoupled agent: **pixels → attribute-rich text → LLM deduction with an explicit logic chain**, plus a **GEAR-131K** data engine.
- Relevant for **reasoning transparency** (the logic chain is textual and inspectable), ORTHOGONAL on mechanism — no relation encoder and no geometric verification.
- All other fields **[U]**.

**SAM-Veteran** — `[F §3.8]` (https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10007418)
- MLLM **agent loops** with SAM for reasoning segmentation; venue verified first-hand (ICLR 2026 program).
- Classified ORTHOGONAL to all nine mechanisms in §5; no architecture, cost or licence facts available.

**4D-RGPT** — `[F §3.8]` (https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Yang_4D-RGPT_Toward_Region-level_4D_Understanding_via_Perceptual_Distillation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)
- Region-level 4D understanding via perceptual distillation; CVPR 2026. ORTHOGONAL — no relation module, no reference token, no ground-plane/building setting.
- All other fields **[U]**.

**Geo-R1** — `[R §2]` (https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/44ecf9a42eed36aecd4969df2ed6d72c0c8db856)
- Existence of "**Geo-R1: Improving Few-Shot Geospatial Referring Expression Understanding with Reinforcement Fine-Tuning**" rests on a **secondary aggregator record only** → all content is **[U]**.
- Recorded because RL-based RS referring is prior art for any RL-shaped contribution, but it cannot be compared mechanism-wise from the raw evidence.

**GeoRel-Prompt** — `[R §2]`, `[R §8]` (https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3821820.3821832)
- Title claims **geo-relational pseudo-label refinement** for referring visual grounding (ACM, DOI 10.1145/3821820.3821832).
- The page is reachable but **Premium/paywalled**, so the method, data and licence are all **[U]**. `[R §8]` explicitly records that a relation-supervision comparison was impossible.
- Because its title occupies "geo-relational", any new naming must be checked against it.

**From Pixels to Semantics (ISPRS Annals 2026)** — `[R §2]`, `[R §7 Q6]`, `[R §8]` (https://isprs-annals.copernicus.org/articles/XI-2-2026/857/2026/isprs-annals-XI-2-2026-857-2026.html)
- An instruction-tuned VLM for unified geospatial **building** analysis — **the only building-specific 2026 item found** in the entire survey.
- Its task formulation and relation-awareness are **`UNVERIFIED`**: the article page returned **navigation chrome only** on repeated fetches, and the Semantic Scholar abstract endpoint failed (`[R §8]`).

**Reasoning Segmentation for Images and Videos: A Survey** — `[F §3.4]`, `[R §3]` (https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18816v1)

## 4. Remote-sensing reasoning segmentation vs ordinary referring segmentation

The three categories are stated separately because the task specification requires the distinction; several works span two of them, and that overlap is named in the "basis" column rather than blurred.

| Work | Category | Basis (evidence) |
|---|---|---|
| **LISA** | **Reasoning segmentation** (task originator) | Introduces reasoning segmentation: an implicit, non-explicit query reasoned over inside the LLM, emitted as text + one `<SEG>` per target (`[F §2.1]`). |
| **LISA++** | **Reasoning segmentation**, extended toward instance segmentation + dialogue | Adds instance segmentation and Segmentation-in-Dialogue to LISA with no structural change; its own claim is instance-level and conversational precision, not new spatial reasoning (`[F §2.2]`). |
| **GSVA** | **Reasoning segmentation** (multi-target + negative targets) | Targets GRES: `{obj1}:[SEG], {obj2}:[SEG]` with `[REJ]` for absent objects; handles "complicated spatial relationships" implicitly (`[F §2.3]`). |

| **PSALM** | **Ordinary (unified) segmentation with a language front-end**, plus zero-shot generalized referring | Input schema = images + task instructions + conditional prompts + mask tokens; supports open-vocabulary, GRES and zero-shot VOS as *tasks*, not as reasoning (`[F §2.6]`). |

| **SegEarth-R2 (+ LaSeRS)** | **Reasoning segmentation** (remote sensing), multi-target | "Reasoning requirement" is one of four LaSeRS evaluation dimensions; implicit-vs-explicit reasoning axis; multi-target queries (`[R §4]`, `[R §5]`). |

**Plain statement of the distinction.**
- **Reasoning segmentation** = the instruction does not name the target explicitly; the model must perform inference (implicit query, multi-step composition, or conversational reference to a previously produced mask) to decide *what* to segment. Surveyed members: LISA, LISA++, GSVA, PixelLM, READ, SegLLM, R²S/3D ReasonSeg, SegEarth-R1, SegEarth-R2, Think2Seg-RS, FIRM, TerraScope, PixDLM/DRSeg, LISAt/GRES, GeoSeg, GEAR-Seg, SAM-Veteran, RemoteReasoner (segmentation-adjacent). Sa2VA *claims* this capability but ships **no** reasoning-segmentation benchmark in its eval set (`[S §7]`).
- **Referring segmentation** = a single explicit referring expression identifies the target ("the red-roofed building on the left"), with no inference step. Surveyed members: RSRefSeg, RECS4R, VideoGLaMM, PhysMLLMs, Geo-R1, GeoRel-Prompt, GeoSearcher (grounding rather than masks), and GeoSelect (an explicit typed spatial program over candidate sets — composition, but no implicit query).
- **Ordinary semantic / instance / panoptic segmentation with a language front-end** = language specifies *categories or task modes*, not an individual referent. Surveyed members: Text4Seg (decoder-free text-as-mask), PSALM (task-instruction + condition-prompt schema), OMG-LLaVA (image/object/pixel-level), UniGeoSeg (unified multi-task), STAMP/STAMPlus (all-mask output interface), SAMTok (mask-language model across many tasks), LISAt to the extent it is instruction-following rather than inferential.
- **Cross-cutting consequence for this audit:** every surveyed *reasoning*-segmentation work either (a) emits one target token per target (LISA, LISA++, GSVA, SegEarth-R2, TerraScope, FIRM, STAMP) or (b) emits explicit geometry (Think2Seg-RS, GeoSeg, GeoSelect) — and **no remote-sensing work in either group has a reference-region token or supervises a reference region** (`[R §7 Q2]`). The only work that does is **SegLLM**, and it operates on natural images.

## 6. Gaps

Each bullet is phrased as a verifiable absence with the evidence that supports it.

- **No work supervises the *relation* geometrically — i.e. stores an explicit spatial-relation triple and re-checks it against predicted region geometry.** The nearest cases are each verifiably something else: Think2Seg-RS uses **mask-IoU-only GRPO** on the final mask (`[R §7 Q1]`, https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19302v2); SegEarth-R2's `L_S` supervises `[SEG]`→patch **attention** derived from the downsampled GT mask (`[R §7 Q4]`, https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.20013); SegLLM supervises the **reference mask** (`[R §7 Q1]`, https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2); FIRM's GT mask codes are a deterministic function of the GT mask (τ = 0.5 sub-cell occupancy) but encode fidelity, not relation (`[R §7 Q1]`, https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13980v1); TerraScope uses Dice + pixel CE on GT masks (`[R §7 Q1]`, https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19039v1). Explicit negative result: "**No work stores or re-checks an explicit spatial-relation triple against predicted region geometry.**"
- **No learned relation encoder that consumes the model's own predicted component masks/boxes.** `[F §4]` closes with: "a **learned relation encoder consuming the model's own predicted component masks/boxes is `NO_EVIDENCE`**". SegLLM's mask-encoding loop is the closest, and it re-injects a CLIP masked-crop embedding plus a mask-derived bounding-box positional embedding **into the LLM** rather than through a relation module (`[R §7 Q3]`).
- **No spatial-consistency loss defined as "the predicted relation must agree with the geometry of the predicted masks".** `[F §4]`: "(d) a spatial-consistency loss defined as 'the predicted relation must agree with the geometry of the predicted masks' is implemented by **none** of them". Every candidate is either attention-space (SegEarth-R2), boundary-gradient (RECS4R), video spatio-temporal (PhysMLLMs), or a plain mask reconstruction loss.
- **A reference-region token with supervised reference-mask decoding is *not* novel in general — but is absent from the entire remote-sensing line.** SegLLM has `[REF]` + `[SEG]` with `L_seg(F([REF],[PAD]), M_ref)` since Oct 2024 (`[R §7 Q2]`, https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2), and the survey's Table 1 independently lists SegLLM as `<SEG>, <REF>` (`[F §3.4]`). In RS: SegEarth-R1 has **no** special token; SegEarth-R2 and TerraScope use `[SEG]` only; FIRM uses `[SEG]`+`[MASK]`; STAMP uses `<SEG>`+mask tokens; Think2Seg-RS uses no tokens (JSON geometry); PixDLM and UniGeoSeg token designs are `UNVERIFIED` → "**In remote sensing: NO / NO_EVIDENCE.**" (`[R §7 Q2]`).
- **A mask-in reference token already exists at CVPR 2026 Highlight level**, so "we insert a region/reference token" is not inherently novel: SAMTok turns any mask into exactly two discrete tokens that can appear **in the prompt**, and OMG-LLaVA's `<Region>` is a de-facto reference-region input (`[F §3.7]`, `[F §5 (B)]`). Neither has reference-mask supervision.
- **No surveyed remote-sensing reasoning-segmentation method specialises in building groups / building-level spatial relations.** Evidence for the absence: SegEarth-R1 uses 28 generic scene categories (Million-AID/fMoW); SegEarth-R2/LaSeRS organises 122 generic RS categories by granularity/multiplicity/reasoning/variability; TerraScope's sub-tasks are land-cover/EO (CA, AQ, CR, BRD, DM, BCE); SegLLM is natural-image only. The only building-focused item found is the ISPRS Annals 2026 paper, and its relation-awareness is `UNVERIFIED` because the page body did not render (`[R §7 Q6]`, `[R §8]`).
- **No Chinese-instruction remote-sensing reasoning-segmentation benchmark was found.** EarthReason's published template is English (`"USER: This is an image <IMAGE>, please doing geospatial pixel reasoning according to the following instruction: <DESCRIPTION>"`), and the Hub language tag could not be verified because the host was unreachable (`[R §7.1]`, https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09644v1).
- **No building-group / reference-building reasoning-segmentation benchmark was found.** `[R §7.1]` records this as "**Not found** (only the ISPRS Annals building-analysis paper, `UNVERIFIED`)".
- **No work chains ≥2 relations as ≥2 separately supervised reference regions inside one instruction.** SegLLM chains across conversational **rounds** (positional/interactional/hierarchical, 2–8 rounds) rather than composing relations within one instruction (`[F §4]`); TerraScope composes L1 questions into multi-step L2-Spatial tasks, but supervision remains final-answer + mask (`[R §7 Q5]`); FIRM's multi-turn referring examples are dialogue-style, not chained relations (`[R §7 Q5]`).
- **No published claim of "first to introduce a reference token", "first spatial relation encoder for segmentation", or a "spatial consistency loss" for reasoning segmentation was found** — a verifiable absence recorded as a negative search result (`[F §5 #14]`), alongside the positive fact that the *terminology* is already occupied: `[REF]` by SegLLM and PSALM, "reference token" by PaDT's VRTs, "spatial consistency" by PhysMLLMs, and "geo-relational" by GeoRel-Prompt.
- **Not a literature gap but a decisive practical gap: no code, weight or dataset licence in this survey is verifiable.** `github.com`, `raw.githubusercontent.com`, `api.github.com`, `huggingface.co` and `pytorch.org` were DNS-blackholed in all three research sessions, so **every** licence cell above is `UNVERIFIED / DO NOT USE UNTIL RESOLVED` (`[F §0]`, `[F §6]`, `[F §8]`; `[S §12]`, `[S §15]`; `[R §0]`, `[R §8]`, `[R §9.1]`). The raw proxy evidence (Sa2VA `LICENSE` = Apache-2.0 text) and every mirror `license:` field are **[PF]** and explicitly inadmissible. Resolve before any redistribution or weight use.
