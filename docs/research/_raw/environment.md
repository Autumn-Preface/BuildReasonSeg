Target machine: Windows 11 x64 · Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX · 32 GB DDR5-5600 · NVIDIA RTX 5080 **Laptop** 16 GB (Blackwell, compute capability **sm_120**) · 1 TB NVMe.
Stack under test: compact VLM fine-tune (Qwen3-VL-2B/4B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-VL-3B/7B-Instruct) + SAM2 (SAM 2.1) mask-decoder head + custom segmentation token, LoRA/QLoRA.
Access date for every source: **2026-09-26**. Source IDs `[Sxx]` resolve in the deduplicated source list at the end.
