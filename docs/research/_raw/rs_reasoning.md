## 7. Six audit questions (evidence-based)
| # | Question | Answer | Which work(s), with evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Any work supervising spatial reasoning with **geometry-verifiable** targets (relation re-checkable geometrically, not judged by a language model)? | **Partially YES — but no work verifies the *relation itself* geometrically.** | **Think2Seg-RS**: supervision is *only* a format reward **+ final mask IoU against the GT mask** — the paper explicitly contrasts this with pseudo box–point supervision → https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19302v2 . **SegEarth-R2**: spatial-attention supervision loss computed from the **downsampled GT mask** → https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.20013 . **SegLLM**: the **reference** mask is itself supervised (`L_seg(F([REF],[PAD]), M_ref)`) → https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2 . **FIRM**: GT mask codes are a deterministic geometric function of the GT mask (sub-cell occupancy ≥ τ=0.5) → https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13980v1 . **TerraScope**: mask loss = Dice + pixel CE on GT masks → https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19039v1 . **No work stores or re-checks an explicit spatial-relation triple against predicted region geometry.** |
| **2** | Explicit **reference region token** (`[REF]`-style) distinct from the target token? | **YES — but only outside remote sensing.** | **SegLLM**: "We generate two tokens `[REF]` and `[SEG]` to the mask decoder. The `[REF]` token contains information about the referenced mask and `[SEG]` token contains information about the relational query", with `L_mask = L_seg(F([REF],[PAD]), M_ref) + L_seg(F([REF],[SEG]), M_tgt)` → https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2 . **In remote sensing: NO / NO_EVIDENCE.** SegEarth-R1 has **no** special token (uses `<DESCRIPTION>` embeddings) → https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09644v1 ; SegEarth-R2 and TerraScope use `[SEG]` only; FIRM uses `[SEG]`+`[MASK]`; STAMP uses `<SEG>` + mask tokens; Think2Seg-RS uses **no tokens** (JSON geometry). PixDLM/UniGeoSeg token designs `UNVERIFIED`. **→ The RS-specific slot is open; the generic mechanism is NOT.** |
| **3** | Explicit **spatial relation encoder** over predicted region geometry? | **NO_EVIDENCE for a *relation* encoder; PARTIAL for a *reference-region* encoder.** | SegLLM's Mask-Encoding Module builds, for each previous mask, a **CLIP-ViT embedding of the cropped masked region + a bounding-box positional embedding** fed back into the LLM — an explicit reference-region encoder, but relations are still resolved by the LLM over text/embedding context, not by a geometric relation module → https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2 . | **4** | **Spatial consistency loss**? | **YES, with a caveat — attention-space only, not geometry-space.** | **SegEarth-R2**: `L_S = -log( Σ(A_S − a)²·Ĝ / ΣĜ )`, maximising separation between `[SEG]`→patch attention on foreground and the average background attention `a`, with `Ĝ` = downsampled GT mask → https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.20013 . SegLLM uses only per-mask CE+DICE (no spatial-consistency term) → https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2 . **No method found with a consistency loss over predicted region geometry or over relation correctness.** |
| **5** | **Multi-hop** spatial reasoning (≥2 chained relations) evaluated? | **YES — two works, one with masks.** | **TerraScope**: Terra-CoT **L2-Spatial** "requires cross-entity spatial analysis such as relationship inference" (e.g. "Is the water adjacent to the crops?"), built by composing multiple L1 questions into complex multi-step tasks, with masks interleaved into the trace; 1 M such samples; dual answer+mask metrics → https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19039v1 . **SegLLM**: chained multi-round relations (2–8 rounds) where each round conditions on **previously segmented masks** — positional ("the class that is <relationship> the output from round <i>"), interactional, and hierarchical (part-of) — plus an MRSeg (hard) split where previous-round information is *necessary* → https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18923v2 . **TerraLogic**: 545 hierarchy-aware long-horizon tasks, **no masks** → https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12497 . | **6** | Which specialise in **building groups / building-level** spatial relations rather than generic land-cover? | **NONE of the surveyed RS reasoning-segmentation methods.** | SegEarth-R1: 28 scene categories (Million-AID/fMoW) — https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09644v1 . SegEarth-R2/LaSeRS: 122 generic RS categories organised by granularity/multiplicity/reasoning/variability, not buildings — https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2512.20013 . SegLLM: natural images, no RS.
